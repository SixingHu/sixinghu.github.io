Sixing Hu 胡思行

I am Sixing Hu, a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research and Analytics from the National University of Singapore in 2025, advised by Yifan Feng and Jussi Keppo.

My research is in operations management, with a focus on the design and management of digital platforms. I combine field experiments, causal inference, and analytical modeling to study how information and incentives shape individual behavior and market outcomes, with implications for platform design and operational decision-making.

Current research

Driver Service Quality and Customer Lifetime Value

Academic–industry collaboration with DiDi · April 2026–present

I study the relationship between driver service quality and subsequent passenger spending using high-dimensional driver–passenger fixed-effects models, with validation through pre-ride spending placebos, cross-period stability, and out-of-sample tests.
